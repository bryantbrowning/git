TEACH THE DOCUMENT #1
Festival guide: teach the choices
Unit 2 · launch Lesson 1; due at Lesson 2's start.

FINAL: one 3–5-minute English recording; visible genuine document; brief cue notes, not full-script reading.
The instructor announces the actual calendar and submission route.

SOURCE SELECTION
A genuine English festival guide, event brochure or credited textbook festival document.

REQUIREMENTS
- Show and credit one real English document; use a readable view.
- Explain its purpose and audience, then teach 3–5 useful vocabulary items and 3–5 key ideas.
- Point to at least two precise source details; distinguish printed information from guesses or missing audio answers.
- Ask at least two comprehension questions and leave a pause before explaining the answers.
- Give a visitor-focused recommendation, one source limitation and a short summary.

WORKED SOURCE
Worked source: the supplied textbook festival brochure, p.42

- The brochure identifies Cambridge, Tokyo and Muscat as festival locations.
- It lists a picnic under cherry trees for Tokyo.
- It lists cycling and camel racing under the Muscat activities.

CREDIT
Unlock, second edition, Level 2 Listening and Speaking · Cambridge University Press · printed pages 42.

VOCABULARY EXAMPLES
- festival: an organized set of events
- activity: something visitors can do
- traditional: connected to older customs

SHORT DEMONSTRATION EXTRACT (not full submission length)
This document is a festival brochure in our textbook. Its purpose is to help us compare festival activities. A festival is an organized set of events. An activity is something visitors can do. Traditional means connected to older customs. Notice the three locations: Cambridge, Tokyo and Muscat. The Tokyo section mentions a picnic under cherry trees. The Muscat section includes cycling and camel racing. Which city is connected to the Cherry Blossom Festival? Pause and check: Tokyo. Which listed Muscat activity involves animals? Camel racing. I would use this document to choose a type of experience, but I would check a current organizer’s programme for dates, prices and availability. The blank spaces require the textbook audio; I have not invented those answers.

COMPREHENSION CHECKS
Which city does the brochure connect to the Cherry Blossom Festival?
Answer: Tokyo.
Which listed Muscat activity involves animals?
Answer: Camel racing.

EVALUATION / SOURCE LIMIT
The brochure is useful for comparing activity types. It is a learning document with audio-completion gaps, not a verified current booking guide. Do not fill the gaps from guesswork.

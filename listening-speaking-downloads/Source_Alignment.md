- **TTD1: Festival guide: teach the choices.** Worked source: Worked source: the supplied textbook festival brochure, p.42. The brochure is useful for comparing activity types. It is a learning document with audio-completion gaps, not a verified current booking guide. Do not fill the gaps from guesswork.

- **TTD2: Persuade a visitor, fairly.** Worked source: Worked source: textbook National Day poster, p.53. For an art-and-history visitor, the fort has relevant features. The textbook poster does not establish a current timetable, price or booking route.

- **TTD3: What the technology can do.** Worked source: Worked source: KeePassXC project README, retrieved15 October2026. These are the project’s feature claims, not an independent security audit. The excerpt does not prove that every user can use it safely or that no data can ever be lost.

- **TTD4: A balanced technology briefing.** Worked source: Worked source: Jitsi Meet project README, retrieved15 October2026. Content sharing may help a remote TTD group, but the excerpt does not establish performance on a particular network or device. Weigh importance, not the number of listed features.

- **TTD5: From research notes to a mini-brief.** Worked source: Worked source: Western Ghats preparation text, textbook p.92. The text gives background for predicting report topics. Current rainfall trends or numerical forecasts need dated regional research; do not present the source as live information.

- **TTD6: Teach a cause–effect pathway.** Worked source: Worked sources: textbook cause–effect guidance pp.96–99. Keep the sea-ice/hunting and land-ice/sea-level pathways separate. Old regional projections in a textbook are not current forecasts; label inferences and seek dated evidence.

They are preserved unchanged in the working Unit1_Reference folder; their original bytes were checked. They are not counted as part of the twelve new lessons. The package does not require following them during class. Publisher book scans come from the user-provided PDFs.

TEACH THE DOCUMENT #2
Persuade a visitor, fairly
Unit 2 · launch Lesson 3; due at Lesson 4's start.

FINAL: one 3–5-minute English recording; visible genuine document; brief cue notes, not full-script reading.
The instructor announces the actual calendar and submission route.

SOURCE SELECTION
A real English event poster or official programme, or the credited textbook National Day poster.

REQUIREMENTS
- Show and credit an event document and name your intended visitor.
- Teach 3–5 useful words and 3–5 ideas through visible source details.
- Make a suggestion and support it with two audience-relevant reasons; save the strongest reason for last.
- Acknowledge one concern or alternative and respond politely.
- Ask two comprehension questions, summarize and identify one booking/detail check.

WORKED SOURCE
Worked source: textbook National Day poster, p.53

- The poster locates its programme in the UAE in December.
- The fort section mentions poetry, drumming and traditional arts.
- The fort section includes making art and trying food.

CREDIT
Unlock, second edition, Level 2 Listening and Speaking · Cambridge University Press · printed pages 53.

VOCABULARY EXAMPLES
- suggest: offer a possible action
- traditional arts: arts connected with established customs
- persuade: give reasons to help someone choose

SHORT DEMONSTRATION EXTRACT (not full submission length)
This is the National Day poster on page 53 of our textbook. It aims to attract visitors to events in the UAE in December. I will explain three words: suggest means offer a possible action; traditional arts are arts connected with established customs; persuade means give reasons to help someone choose. The fort section mentions poetry and drumming. It also says visitors can try traditional arts, make art and eat food. What month does the poster name? December. Name one activity a visitor can try. Making art is one answer. For someone who likes art and history, I would suggest the fort because they can join in as well as watch. However, this is a textbook poster, and we need a current timetable and price before making a real visit. My recommendation follows the visitor’s interests, rather than claiming the same event suits everyone.

COMPREHENSION CHECKS
What month does this poster name?
Answer: December.
Name one activity a visitor can try at the fort.
Answer: Making art / traditional arts; the poster also mentions food.

EVALUATION / SOURCE LIMIT
For an art-and-history visitor, the fort has relevant features. The textbook poster does not establish a current timetable, price or booking route.

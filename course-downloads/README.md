# Unlock Units 2–4 · Weekly Project Studio

[**Download the updated twelve-lesson course**](./Unlock_Units_2-4_12_Lessons.zip?raw=true) · [Preview the six weekly projects](./Interaction_Preview.jpg) · [Preview all twelve designs](./Design_Preview.jpg)

Download and extract the ZIP, then open **index.html** inside **Unlock_Units_2-4**. Choose a lesson and present it in a modern browser. These are standalone HTML presentations with JavaScript built in.

All twelve lessons now follow the six Unit 2–4 project cycles in **XXU_TTD_Writing.docx**. Each assignment has a clear checklist, illustrated example, worked organizer, writing model and teaching route. Unit 1 in the document is outside this package.

| Week | Lessons | Teach the Document | Required writing |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 2.1–2.2 | Festival Field Guide: Beyond China | 7+ sentences |
| 2 | 2.3–2.4 | Festival Museum: Three Objects, One Story | 8–10 sentences |
| 3 | 3.1–3.2 | The Privacy Survival Guide | 1–2 paragraphs, 8–12 sentences total |
| 4 | 3.3–3.4 | Internet Time: A Clear Verdict | 8–10 sentences; three supporting ideas |
| 5 | 4.1–4.2 | Postcards from Around the World | 1–2 paragraphs, 8–12 sentences total |
| 6 | 4.3–4.4 | Climate Doppelgängers | 1–2 paragraphs, 8–12 sentences total |

The four substantial replacements are now complete project workflows:

- **Museum:** three illustrated exhibit cards, source-supported descriptions and connections, a coherent explanatory paragraph and a museum-tour video.
- **Privacy guide:** a risk/harm/protection organizer, priorities, a creative one-page guide, opinion writing and the required video/read-aloud route.
- **Climate postcards:** a city outside China, a four-part organizer, four original illustrated postcards, a full annual climate graph, descriptive writing and video.
- **Climate doppelgängers:** two cities, monthly climate data and English reading, a supported similarity, three meaningful comparisons, four numerical observations across both measures, an original investigation visual, comparison writing and video.

**Project → Create** stays inside the current slide and offers **Example / Example plan / Build mine / Preview mine / Requirements**. Students can edit the document, add local images to museum cards or postcards, generate annual graphs from monthly data, and export the organizer and creative document as standalone printable HTML. Complete models remain available within each presentation; separate illustrated example pages are optional.

The updated checklists reflect the Word document. Class revision receipts, suggested rubrics and paired rehearsal timing support learning without adding extra submission requirements. The privacy-guide model and its standalone export were checked to print on one A4 page.

Every lesson keeps its 90-minute plan and 32 timed activities. The full course retains 384 activities in 318 integrated core canvases, plus 64 optional extensions, twelve distinct visual designs, twelve paper worksheets and six TTD workshop sheets. The textbook sources, annotation, sentence-role study, writing studio, revision tools and local video player remain on the same canvas.

Earlier Writing Studio v2 JSON exports remain compatible. Earlier drafts and notes are kept; obsolete completion checks reset so they do not mark the changed task complete. Export editable work before closing or moving devices. Student writing and local media are not uploaded by the presentation files.
